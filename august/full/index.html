What This Month Tells Us
August was a normal shoulder-season month, yet it pushed Pure Kauai to the best goal-pace on record: $8.79M identified, 92.5% of the $9.5M goal, with only $708K left to close. Web traffic and phone calls both turned positive year over year, and the Google Ads account was restructured onto US and high-intent geo targeting. The Deep Dash, ads, calls, GA4 and AEO detail all refresh below.

$737K of accommodations revenue for August check-ins, essentially level with August 2025 (−0.1%). A shoulder month after July's $1.47M peak; the strength is in the YTD pace, not the single month.

How to read forward bookings
Forward bookings represent paid or confirmed upcoming stays. The gap between Identified ($8.79M) and Completed YTD ($6.38M) = $2.41M in confirmed future revenue. As trips complete through the year, Completed will rise toward Identified — and new bookings keep adding to both.

Why GA4 revenue ($324K) and Hostfully revenue ($737K) disagree
GA4 attributes $324K of revenue to web sessions in August. Hostfully shows $737K of accommodations revenue for August check-ins. The gap is the offline / agent-assisted side of the business — guests who find Pure Kauai digitally, then call or email a reservation agent and complete the booking outside the website's tracked funnel. This is the signature of a relationship-driven luxury rental brand. Direct ($191K) and "Unassigned" ($70K) are the largest GA4 buckets precisely because the booking lifecycle frequently leaves the website mid-funnel.
