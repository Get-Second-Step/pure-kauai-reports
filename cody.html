Why conversions appeared in the last week of April Google Ads attributes a booking only after the purchase is confirmed by the booking platform. Several bookings made earlier in April were finalized during the last week — that's when the conversion data settled. This is normal behavior for high-value vacation rental bookings which often involve a deposit followed by a final payment confirmation. The 3 verified bookings ($111,112) are solid. The 4 in pipeline ($206,629 attributed) will be confirmed or dropped as those guest inquiries are resolved.
Why Google Ads shows 10 events but Google Analytics shows 28 purchases Google Ads only credits bookings it can link directly to an ad click. Google Analytics counts all purchases from all sources — organic search, direct, referral, ads. Both are correct. The $111,112 confirmed through Google Ads is a floor, not a ceiling — many guests click an ad first, then return directly to book days later, which Google Ads does not capture.
California campaign — needs review before May California spent $1,763.28 and shows 2 purchase events ($124,017 attributed) — but those bookings are still in pipeline, not confirmed in Hostfully. Worth checking whether these 2 guests are in the inquiry queue and whether the landing page experience for California traffic matches what they're expecting.

4  ·  Tracking Fix — Resolved
SystemWhat Was WrongStatusImpact Going Forward
CallRail → Google Ads During April, calls and forms were not being reported back to Google Ads as conversions. Now fixed. Fixed ✓ Google's Smart Bidding can now learn from phone leads — better ad targeting from May onwards
What this means in plain terms When a guest calls Pure Kauai after seeing a Google Ad, that call is now reported back to Google automatically. Google uses this signal to get smarter about who to show ads to. This fix did not affect April's $111,112 in tracked booking value — those came through the website. But it will improve performance in May and beyond.
5  ·  What's Working — Keep Doing This
  • Best April revenue pacing ever recorded. At 70.3% of the $9.5M goal by April 13 — ahead of every prior year. Forward bookings ($4.61M) are up 29% vs April 2025.
  • Google Ads is delivering strong returns. $111,112 in confirmed booking value on $6,405 in spend = 17.3× return. Two underperforming campaigns (PMax and Phone Calls) have been paused — saving ~$1,258/month going forward.
  • 89.6% of April bookings came through direct channels — not VRBO or Airbnb. This means Pure Kauai keeps the OTA commission (typically 15–20%) on those stays. The website and Google Ads are contributing to this direct booking ratio.
  • Organic search is driving calls and bookings. 21 of 58 April calls came from Google organic search. The island guide content is working as a top-of-funnel driver.
  • BookingBridge is live and matched. The attribution system linking website ad clicks to Hostfully bookings is operational. 72 bookings in the last 6 months have been traced back to a marketing source.
