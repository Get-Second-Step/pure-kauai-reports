4 Tracking Fix — One Action Required
Full action list →
  • CallRail is not communicating with Google Ads. When someone calls after seeing an ad, that call should be reported back to Google Ads so it can learn what's working. Right now, that connection is broken.
  • This is a one-time settings fix inside CallRail. Once done, Google Ads will get smarter about which ads to show to people who are likely to call — which should improve performance over time.
  • This does not affect the current ad results ($111K tracked). It affects how well Google can optimize future ad spend.
Who handles this This is a settings fix in CallRail — Wendy or the team can action it, or Second Step can walk through it on the next call. It takes about 10 minutes once we're in the account together.
