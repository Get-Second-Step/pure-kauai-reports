Tracking gap: Google Ads calls missing GCLID
8 calls were attributed to Google Ads by CallRail (via UTM or session signals) but 0 of these have a GCLID attached. GCLID is the click identifier that links a Google Ads click to a call conversion. Without it, these 8 calls cannot be attributed back to specific campaigns or keywords in Google Ads, and Smart Bidding cannot learn from them. This needs to be fixed in the CallRail ↔ Google Ads integration settings.

Priority action items — April 2026
  • 1
    Fix the missed call problem — 45/58 calls unanswered Options: (a) Add a team member for call coverage during business hours; (b) Enable CallRail's SMS auto-response for missed calls — "Sorry we missed you, text us or visit purekauai.com/properties"; (c) Set up voicemail capture with prompt callbacks. At $27K average booking value, each recovered call = major revenue.
  • 2
    Fix GCLID tracking in CallRail — 8 Google Ads calls missing attribution In CallRail: Settings → Integrations → Google Ads. Verify the "Import call conversions" toggle is ON and the Google Ads account is connected. Without this, Google Ads Smart Bidding cannot learn from phone calls, and the Phone Calls campaign's spend cannot be justified.
  • 3
    PMax & Phone Calls paused — good. Now reallocate the $1,258/mo. Both campaigns are now paused (confirmed via Google Ads API). PMax spent $1,089 and Phone Calls $169 before being paused in April.     Audit the April 13 call spike — what caused 15 calls in one day? Unusual volume on April 13. Check: Did a blog post rank? Was there a social post? Did a travel advisor send traffic?
