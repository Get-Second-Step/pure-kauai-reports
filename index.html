Attribution note: Google Ads vs GA4 show different numbers — both are correct
Google Ads shows 3 purchases = $111,112. GA4 shows 28 purchases = $893,205. These are not the same metric. Google Ads uses last-click / data-driven attribution and only counts conversions it can attribute to an ad click. GA4 counts all purchases across all channels (Direct, Organic, Paid Search, etc.) using a session-based model. A guest who clicked a Google Ad but returned 3 days later to book directly will appear as Direct in GA4 but as a Google Ads conversion in the ad platform. Both views are accurate — they're measuring different things.

Why "Direct" dominates GA4 — this is expected for luxury travel
71% of GA4-attributed revenue shows as Direct traffic. This is very common for high-consideration purchases like luxury vacation rentals. A guest may first find Pure Kauai via Google Ads or organic search, then return directly multiple times over days or weeks before booking. GA4 credits the last session — which is often Direct. This does not mean ads aren't working. It means the conversion window is longer than a single session. BookingBridge and Google Ads data (17.2x ROAS) tell the fuller story.
