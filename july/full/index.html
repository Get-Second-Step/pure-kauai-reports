How to read forward bookings
Forward bookings represent paid or confirmed upcoming stays. The gap between Identified ($8.10M) and Completed YTD ($5.61M) = $2.49M in confirmed future revenue. As trips complete through the year, Completed will rise toward Identified — and new bookings keep adding to both.

Why GA4 revenue ($581K) and Hostfully revenue ($1.47M) disagree
GA4 attributes $581K of revenue to web sessions in July. Hostfully shows $1.47M of accommodations revenue for July check-ins. The gap is the offline / agent-assisted side of the business — guests who find Pure Kauai digitally, then call or email a reservation agent and complete the booking outside the website's tracked funnel. This is the signature of a relationship-driven luxury rental brand. Direct ($250K) and "Unassigned" ($205K) are the largest GA4 buckets precisely because the booking lifecycle frequently leaves the website mid-funnel.
