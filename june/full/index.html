Why GA4 revenue ($552K) and Hostfully revenue ($853K) disagree
GA4 attributes $279K of revenue to web sessions in June. Hostfully shows $1.08M of accommodations revenue for June check-ins. The gap is the offline / agent-assisted side of the business — guests who find Pure Kauai digitally, then call or email a reservation agent and complete the booking outside the website's tracked funnel. This is the signature of a relationship-driven luxury rental brand. "Unassigned" ($95K) and Direct ($69K) remain the largest GA4 buckets precisely because the booking lifecycle frequently leaves the website mid-funnel.
