What this means Pure Kauai has identified $7.81M of 2026 accommodations revenue through Jun 30 — 82.2% of the $9.5M goal and the strongest pace on record at this point in the year, ahead of 2025's prior-best 80.6% and +$553K ahead in absolute dollars. That is $4.15M of completed stays plus $3.66M already forward-booked across 125 future trips. June itself brought in $1.08M of accommodations revenue, the second-strongest completed month of 2026 — modestly below a strong June 2025 (−6.5%) but well inside a record year. July is already on the books at $1.43M (+14% vs July 2025).

California + Secret Beach burned $4,602 with zero confirmed conversions Pure Kauai California ($2,181), PMax California Target ($1,474) and Secret Beach ($947) combined for 67% of June spend and produced no tracked conversions — the same pattern as May, now more pronounced. PMax California's 0.64% CTR is the weakest signal in the account. Two angles: clicks landing but not converting (landing page or audience mismatch), or conversions completing later through Direct/Organic and going un-credited. A California audit is the priority before next month's spend.
Only 3 conversions tracked on $6,915 of spend June logged 3 confirmed conversions account-wide, all from Geo and Brand. Volume is thin because Google Ads only counts confirmed BookingBridge purchases; the GA4 + BookingBridge cross-check now in progress is what will surface any California assists. Until then, hold the California campaigns to a test budget and keep funding Geo and Brand, which are doing all the measurable work.

6  ·  What's Working — Keep Doing This
  • Strongest pace on record. $7.81M identified through Jun 30 is 82.2% of the $9.5M goal — the best position at this point in any tracked year (2025's prior best was 80.6%) and +$553K ahead of 2025 in absolute dollars.
  • Geo returned 18.8× and Brand 76×. The Geo campaign delivered $41,109 on $2,184 and 2 of the account's 3 conversions; Brand stayed the most efficient dollar at $9,834 on $129. Keep the budget on these two.
  • Hostfully direct dominates the channel mix. Per the Deep Dash breakdown, Hostfully accounts for roughly 88% of 2026 booking value ($6.96M rent + fees) versus $609K Vrbo and $287K Airbnb — keeping the 15–20% OTA commission with the business on the vast majority of stays.
  • Calls from Google Organic tripled year-over-year. 15 of June's 25 calls came from Google Organic, up +200% vs June 2025. The island-guide content is doing real top-of-funnel work that converts straight to phone bookings.
  • AI Visibility is climbing. 26/100 in June, up from 23 in May, with 695 AI citations across 138 cited pages. Still Low, but the AEO foundation is starting to compound.
  • High-intent behavior keeps improving. Purchases 21 (+50% YoY), form submits 82 (+52% YoY), add-to-cart 69, and Paid Search attributed revenue up +$56K YoY to the #2 channel. Fewer visitors are doing more.
