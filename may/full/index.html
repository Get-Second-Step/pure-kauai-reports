Annual goal hit by end of May — $10.67M identified against the $9.5M target (112.3%)

As of May 31, Pure Kauai has $4.61M in completed stays plus $6.06M forward-booked into the rest of the year — 340 trips on the books. May added 41 new bookings worth $1.17M, up 46% in volume and 29% in revenue versus May 2025. The telecom forwarding issue that caused April's busy-signal problem is resolved; CallRail is now reaching the reservations team. The next chapter of growth is no longer about hitting the goal — it is about expanding it.

What We Built This Month
May was the first clean month of measurement after April's tracking rebuild. With the foundation set, we shifted focus to the next growth lever: getting Pure Kauai into the answers AI assistants and Google give to luxury Kauai travelers.
AEO foundation shipped
Property-level meta, structured data, and the llms.txt file went live so ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity can confidently cite Pure Kauai properties when travelers ask for recommendations.
38-blog content pipeline launched
First batch of long-form guide content shipped to capture high-intent Kauai planning queries. Built to do work for years, not weeks.
Telecom + CallRail loop closed
Hawaiian Telecom forwarding fixed mid-May. Calls now reach the reservations team and CallRail is logging clean data for the first month in a row.

Reading this honestly
2026's identified revenue by May is the highest absolute number on record at $10.67M. Trip count (340) is the highest ever for this point in the year. Average revenue per booking ($31,371) is below 2025 ($36,593) and 2023 ($36,064) — this is mix shift, not pricing weakness. More mid-tier and shoulder-season trips are entering the funnel alongside the flagship estates. Volume + AOV together still net positive.

How to read forward bookings
Forward bookings represent paid or confirmed upcoming stays. The gap between Identified ($10.67M) and Completed YTD ($4.61M) = $6.06M in confirmed future revenue. As trips complete through the year, Completed will rise toward Identified — and new bookings keep adding to both.

Telecom fix is holding
All nine tracking numbers logged calls this month. None show a busy-signal pattern. Compare this to April, where 45 of 58 inbound calls hit a busy signal because Hawaiian Telecom was misrouting forwarding. The May 19 fix is working as designed.
Short call durations on most numbers
Several tracking numbers show 17–34 second average durations. That is below the 1–2 minute range you would expect for a real inquiry. Two possibilities: (1) many of these are repeat callers who already have what they need and are confirming small details, or (2) some calls are dropping before the reservations team engages. The team should listen back to a sample of the 808-582-8904 pool to confirm.

Why GA4 revenue ($552K) and Hostfully revenue ($853K) disagree
GA4 attributes $552K of revenue to web sessions in May. Hostfully shows $853K of stays completed in May. The $300K gap is the offline / agent-assisted side of the business — guests who find Pure Kauai digitally, then call or email a reservation agent, and complete the booking outside the website's tracked funnel. This is the signature of a relationship-driven luxury rental brand. "Unassigned" ($269K) is the largest GA4 bucket precisely because the booking lifecycle frequently leaves the website mid-funnel.

Quality went up while volume came down
Add-to-cart events tracked 113 in May vs <1 a year ago. Forms +9%, calls +71%, purchases +14% — all up YoY. The site is doing more booking work per visitor, which is the entire point of the April tracking rebuild and the May AEO foundation. Volume will follow once the content pipeline matures.
The traffic drop is real and needs investigation
10,725 sessions in May 2026 vs ~24,000 in May 2025 is a meaningful drop. Most likely culprits, in priority order: (1) a Google algo / SERP layout shift hitting general "Kauai" queries; (2) a 2025 content piece that previously drove a traffic spike that is no longer indexed; (3) competitor displacement on key terms. Search Console drilldown is the next pull.
