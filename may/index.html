What this means Pure Kauai has identified $7.22M of 2026 accommodations revenue through Jun 2 — the highest absolute number ever recorded at this point in the year ($353K ahead of 2025, $311K ahead of 2023). Pacing as a % of goal (76.0%) is essentially tied with the same point in 2025 (76.3%), against a tougher target ($9.5M vs $9.0M). May 2026 was the standout: $788K of accommodations revenue versus $356K in May 2025 (+121%) — the largest YoY month-over-month gain of any 2026 month so far. With $4.09M already forward-booked across 139 future stays, the path to clearing the $9.5M goal is well-defined.

5  ·  What's Working — Keep Doing This
  • Strongest May on record. $788K of May accommodations revenue is +121% versus May 2025 ($356K) — the largest YoY month-over-month gain of any 2026 month. YTD identified of $7.22M is the highest absolute number ever recorded at this point in the year.
  • Brand returned 458× on $129 of spend. The single most efficient dollar in the account. Currently capped by impression share — fund it to whatever the auction will absorb.
  • Hostfully direct dominates the 2026 channel mix. Per the DeepDash breakdown, Hostfully accounts for $6.72M of 2026 booking value (rent + fees) versus $619K Vrbo, $287K Airbnb — keeping the 15–20% OTA commission with the business on the vast majority of stays.
  • Calls from Google Organic doubled year-over-year. 8 calls in May 2026 vs 4 in May 2025. The island-guide content (best month to go, where to see turtles, where to surf) is doing real top-of-funnel work that now converts to phone bookings.
  • Telecom forwarding fix is holding. All nine tracking numbers logged calls; none showed a busy-signal pattern. May is the first clean month since the May 19 fix and the data confirms the issue is fully resolved.
  • Conversion behavior is improving even as traffic drops. Sessions are down 56% year-over-year, but add-to-cart events are up 11,200% YoY, forms +9%, calls +71%, purchases +14%. Fewer visitors are doing more — exactly what the tracking rebuild and AEO foundation are designed to produce.
